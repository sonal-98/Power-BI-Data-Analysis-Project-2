# Hotel Booking & Operations Analysis

## Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI Hotel Booking & Operations Analytics Dashboard** developed to analyze hotel bookings, cancellations, guest behavior, revenue-related metrics, and operational performance.

The project converts hotel booking data into business-oriented dashboards for understanding booking patterns, customer behavior, hotel performance, and operational trends.

## Dataset

The analysis is based on hotel booking data containing information related to:

* Booking status and cancellations
* Hotel type
* Guest/customer information
* Stay duration
* Room and booking characteristics
* ADR (Average Daily Rate)
* Distribution and booking channels
* Customer preferences
* Market segments

## Dashboard 1 — Hotel Performance and Revenue Analytics

Focuses on overall booking performance and Revenue patterns.

### Key KPIs

* Total Bookings
* Realized Revenue
* Cancellation Metrics
* Average Daily Rate (ADR)
* Average Stay

### Key Analysis

* Revenue trends over time
* Booking and cancellation patterns
* Distribution and booking channels
* Revenue by Market segment performance

### Filters

* Hotel Type
* Arrival Year
* Arrival Month
* Market Segment
* Country

## Dashboard 2 — Customer and Booking Behavior

Focuses on overall customer performance and Booking patterns.


### Key Analysis

* Average stay & Booking patterns by customer type
* Total nights by customer type
* Bookings by Distribution channel
* Average ADR by month
  
### Filters

* Hotel Type
* Arrival Month
* Market Segment
* Distribution channel
* Arrival Year

### Interactive Features

* KPI cards
* Interactive charts
* Slicers/filters
* Business-oriented calculated measures

To analyze hotel booking and operational data and convert it into **actionable business insights around bookings, cancellations, customer behavior, revenue indicators, channels, and hotel performance**.
